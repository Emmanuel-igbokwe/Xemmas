How the two books fit together

A data center is the most demanding building a project team will deliver. Once it is open, it becomes a facility that someone has to keep alive.

Build it

Schedules, procurement, change orders and commissioning, written for the person responsible for the whole project.

Keep it running

Maintenance strategy, condition assessment, work orders and lifecycle cost for the years after turnover.

Prove it is safe

Inspection methods, life safety, accessibility and environmental health, with findings that can be closed out.

About the author

Emmanuel Igbokwe is a project manager and engineer based in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, with experience in capital projects, MEP and industrial equipment delivery, and public-sector facility programs. He holds graduate degrees in engineering from LSU and Southern University.

These books are practical guides for learning and planning. They are not legal, engineering or code-compliance advice. Always confirm requirements with your local authority.
